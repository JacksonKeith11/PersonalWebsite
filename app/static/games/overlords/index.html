Overlords

An auto-battler for two to four players. Recruit units from your shop, stack up their traits, place them on your board, and watch them fight everyone else's. Last overlord standing wins.

How to play
  • Each round starts with planning: buy units from your shop (click a card), reroll it for 2 gold, or buy 4 XP for 4 gold. Your level is how many units you can field, and higher levels find pricier units.
  • Drag units between your bench and the bottom half of the board. Drop one on the shop to sell it.
  • Three copies of a unit merge into a two-star; three two-stars make a three-star.
  • Every unit has an origin and one or two classes. Field enough different units sharing one and its trait switches on (see the list on the right).
  • Then everyone fights at once. Your units move and attack on their own, and cast their ability when their mana fills. Lose and you lose HP: more for every enemy left standing.
  • Some rounds are monster rounds, everyone against the same monsters, for gold.
  • You earn gold every round, plus 1 for every 10 you've saved (up to 5), plus a bonus for a winning or losing streak.
  • Click a player on the left to watch their fight, or to see their board while you plan.
  • Monster rounds drop item components. Drag one onto a unit: two components on one unit combine into a stronger item, and a unit holds three. A Sigil with another component makes an emblem (its holder gains that trait); two Sigils make a Crown, one more place on your board.
  • At the start you pick a commander: a passive, and a power that charges over a few rounds (use it from the left panel while planning).
  • At rounds 3, 8 and 13 you pick one of three augments; at rounds 4, 9 and 14 everyone drafts a unit holding a component from a shared set, the lowest HP picking first.
  • Keys: D reroll · F buy XP · E sell the unit under the pointer · Space ready.
