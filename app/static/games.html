Roguelike Tactics Pixel-art strategy

Play in browser

Lead a warrior, a mage and a rogue through three acts of branching battles, strange events and shops. Fights play out on a pixel-art tactics grid with walls, lava and high ground. Artifacts rewrite your skills in odd ways and build toward poison, elemental, shield, thorns, blood or time, and each act boss promotes every hero into something stranger: eighteen classes in all. Built with Phaser, with chiptune music made in code.

Works with a mouse or a touchscreen. Runs save in your browser between visits.

Frag Arena Multiplayer arena shooter

Play in browser

Quake-style deathmatch for whoever's online: everyone who opens the page drops into the same arena. Rockets, a shotgun and a machine gun, plus rare power weapons that turn up around the map: a railgun, a black-hole gun, an orb that zaps everyone near it, a gun that swaps places with whoever it hits, and a yeet cannon. Counter-Strike bunny-hopping, rocket jumps, jump pads. Four maps, from a warehouse of catwalks to a stepped spire, and party modes: Gun Game, Infection and Juggernaut, with mutators like low gravity, instagib and big heads. Add bots to practise alone. Built with three.js, with the game run on this site's server.

Needs a keyboard and mouse. Best with friends on at the same time.

Board Brawl Party board game for friends

Play in browser

A spooky Mario Party-style board game for two to four friends, each on their own phone or computer. Roll around Haunted Hollow, buy crowns, and ruin each other's night with traps, spider webs, curses, hexes, zombie hands and pumpkin bombs, while a ghost hunts whoever's winning. Every round ends in a minigame, free-for-all, two against two, or one against everyone: thirty-nine of them, from bumper cars, air hockey and four-way pong to trivia, draw-and-guess, a rhythm game and two in 3D, many of them takes on Mario Party favourites. Make a room and send your friends the code.

Works on phones and computers. Best with everyone on a call. Bots can fill empty seats.

Saltbound Co-op strategy RPG for 1 to 3 players

Play in browser

A co-op voyage across a sinking archipelago. Pick three heroes from ten classes, plan your moves together across a hex map, and fight with dice: roll every round, then place them on actions while the enemies show what they're about to do. Clear three dungeons and climb the last peak to face the Drowned Crown before the rising sea swallows it. Towns, shrines, wrecks, events, a shared pool of revives, and a new skill to pick each time the party levels up. Runs save as you go.

Best on a computer, with friends on a call. Make a room and share the code, or play all three heroes yourself.

Song Guesser Name that tune

Play in browser

Fifteen seconds of a song, rebuilt note by note with a synthesizer, starting with the drums alone. Each wrong guess adds an instrument (bass, then guitar, then the vocal line) until the whole band is playing. You get the genre and the year. Mostly indie rock, from Weezer to Neutral Milk Hotel and Interpol, with some other songs mixed in.

Turn your sound on. Everything you hear is synthesized in the browser.
