Scramble

A race over an obstacle course that's different every round. Jump the gaps, duck the saws, ride the lifts, and get to the flag before everyone else.

How to play
  • One to eight racers. Bots can fill empty lanes.
  • Run with the arrow keys or A and D, jump with Space, W or the up arrow. Hold jump for a higher jump. On a phone, use the buttons on the screen.
  • In the air, dash with X, S or the down arrow (Dash on a phone): a quick burst forward, once per jump.
  • Touch a spike or a saw, or fall in a pit, and you start that section again from its flag.
  • Other racers can't block you: you run through each other.
  • Run through a ? box for an item, and use it with E or Shift (or the Use button on a phone). The further back you are, the nastier the item: a bolt that knocks out the racer ahead, a freeze for everyone in front, a curse that flips the controls of the racer ahead, a spike trap, a grapple that pulls you up to the racer ahead, or a swap that trades places with them. Bolts and freezes take a moment to arrive: if one's coming for you and you hold a shield, use it. Shields block the next item used on you; boosts make you faster for a moment.
  • Press 1 to 6 for an emote. Once you've finished, the arrow keys switch who you're watching.
  • Some places play differently: snow is icy (slow to get going, slow to stop), sand slows your running (hop to keep your speed), and the caverns are dark.
  • Once the first racer finishes, everyone else has 25 seconds.
  • Each round gives points by place. Most points after the last round wins.
