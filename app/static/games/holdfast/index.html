Holdfast

Tower defence for one to four players. You all build on the same map, but each of you has your own purse, so decide together who pays for what. Hold the keep for thirty waves.

Start a game

Make a room. Play alone, or send friends its code.

Join friends

Type the code from whoever made the room.

How to play
  • Enemies walk the road to the keep. Each one that gets there costs a life (a Warlord costs five). You share twenty.
  • Pick a tower from the bar, then click or tap a free square to build it. Click a tower to see its kills and upgrade it (anyone can pay) or sell it (only its builder).
  • Every tower has two upgrade paths of three tiers. The first upgrade picks the path for good. At the end of each path is a final evolution: expensive, a lot stronger, and only one of each can stand on the map at a time.
  • Every kill pays everyone equally. If a friend is short, give them gold from the players list.
  • Flyers ignore the road and go straight for the keep; cannons and beacons can't touch them.
  • Shieldbearers shrug off small hits. Ballistas go straight through armour.
  • Between waves, press Ready to send the next one early.
