Overlords

An auto-battler for two to four players. Recruit units from your shop, stack up their traits, place them on your board, and watch them fight everyone else's. Last overlord standing wins.

How to play
  • Each round starts with planning: buy units from your shop (click a card), reroll it for 2 gold, or buy 4 XP for 4 gold. Your level is how many units you can field, and higher levels find pricier units.
  • Drag units between your bench and the bottom half of the board. Drop one on the shop to sell it. On a phone you can also tap a unit, then tap where it should go; its card has a Sell button.
  • Three copies of a unit merge into a two-star; three two-stars make a three-star. Every unit has its own ability, and at two and three stars you choose an extra one for it: one for each of its traits, or a stronger version of its own. A gold + marks a unit still waiting for its pick; click or tap it to choose from its card.
  • Every unit has an origin and one or two classes. Field enough different units sharing one and its trait switches on (see the list on the right).
  • Then everyone fights at once. Your units move and attack on their own, and cast their ability when their mana fills. Lose and you lose HP: more for every enemy left standing.
  • Some rounds are monster rounds, everyone against the same monsters, for gold.
  • Once every person in the room is knocked out, the match ends: the bots don't play it out.
  • You earn gold every round, plus 1 for every 8 you've saved (up to 5, so saving 40 earns the most), plus a bonus for a winning or losing streak. Fights against players pay too: 1 gold for a win, and spoils, 1 gold for every 3 of their units you bring down (up to 3).
  • The bar above the shop has everything worth a glance: the round and its timer, your HP, gold (and next round's income), level and XP, how many units you field, your streak and who you fight next. Above every unit, a green bar shows its health (a notch every 300 HP) and a blue one its mana (a notch every 10, an attack's worth): it casts when the blue bar fills.
  • The Guide (here on the menu, and top right in a game) has the rules, the numbers, every trait, unit, commander, item and augment.
  • Click a player on the left to watch their fight, or to see their board while you plan.
  • While you plan, the player you'll fight next is marked in the list, and their board shows faded on the top half of yours, where it will stand when the fight starts: place your units against it (hover or tap one of theirs to read it). On monster rounds the monsters show there.
  • Buy items in the armory, beside your item bench, and drag one onto a unit (or click it, then the unit); a unit holds three. Items come in four rarities: Common (plain stats, 2 gold), Rare (a trick and a stat, 4), Epic (a stronger trick and two stats, or an emblem: its holder gains a trait, 6) and Legendary (artifacts, and the Crown: one more place on your board, 9). Rarer items turn up as the rounds go on; the armory restocks every round. Click or tap a unit to take its items off again.
  • Every unit has its own trick: some taunt, pull, shove, vanish, mark their targets or leave burning ground. Hover a unit, a shop card or a trait to read about it (on a phone, tap a unit or trait, or press and hold a shop card).
  • A unit's card shows its numbers as it would start the next fight, with what traits, items, augments and your commander add in green beside each (hover a number for what it means), each of its traits and what it's doing for it, its ability's damage with its ability power counted, and what each of its items does. The trait list says what each trait gives right now, or what it would give once it's on.
  • During a fight the right panel charts each unit's damage dealt, taken and healed; it keeps the last fight while you plan.
  • At the start you pick a commander: a passive, and a power that charges over a few rounds (use it from the left panel while planning). The first fight waits until everyone has picked.
  • At rounds 3, 8 and 13 you pick one of three augments; at rounds 4, 9 and 14 everyone drafts a unit holding an item (Rare, then Epic, then a Legendary artifact) from a shared set, the lowest HP picking first. Some augments grow with your board: so much for each Mystic you field, say, or your units' extra HP turned into attack. The augment list says what each of those gives you right now.
  • Rounds 6 and 11 close an act with an auction: three lots (a two-star unit holding an item, a relic you can't get anywhere else, and an auction augment). Everyone bids at once, in secret, on any or all of them, each bid up to all your gold. The highest bid takes each lot and pays what it bid, but nobody wins more than one, so you never pay twice. Saved gold buys you power there.
  • Exit (top right) leaves a match for good: you're knocked out on the spot.
  • Keys: D reroll · F buy XP · E sell the unit under the pointer · Space ready.
