Chicken Stir Fry

Ingredients

  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp corn starch
  • 1/2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/4 tsp crushed red pepper
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 4 cups veggies
  • 2 cloves or 2 tsp minced garlic
  • 1 lb chicken

Steps

  1. Mix the first 5 ingredients and set aside.
  2. Heat oil in a skillet, add the veggies and garlic, and cook until tender (about 5 minutes). Take them out.
  3. Heat oil, add the chicken, and cook until done. Add the veggies back in.
  4. Stir in the broth mixture, bring to a boil, and cook about 5 minutes.

Chicken Meatballs

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground chicken
  • 1/2 cup panko bread crumbs
  • 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg
  • Spices: salt, pepper, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, onion powder

Steps

  1. Mix everything together and roll into balls.
  2. Cook in a pan with oil, or bake at 400°F for about 15 minutes.

Good with a lemon butter sauce or a red sauce.

Lemon butter pasta

  1. Boil the pasta separately.
  2. In a pan, melt butter with some garlic and onion (powder works), add Parmesan, and squeeze in half a lemon.
  3. Add the pasta and the cooked meatballs.

Teriyaki Chicken Fried Rice

Ingredients

  • 4 cups rice, cooked the night before and chilled
  • 10 eggs
  • Chicken, sliced into pieces
  • Teriyaki sauce
  • 1 broccoli crown
  • 1/3 spring onion
  • Vegetable oil
  • Butter
  • Soy sauce
  • To taste: white pepper, MSG, garlic salt, toasted sesame oil

Steps

  1. The night before, cook the rice and leave it in the fridge.
  2. Heat vegetable oil in a large skillet, add the rice, and add butter as it begins to fry.
  3. Once the rice is frying, add the eggs.
  4. In a separate pan, heat more vegetable oil and cook the chicken until it's no longer pink. Top with teriyaki sauce.
  5. Add the broccoli and spring onion to the rice, adding more oil as it fries.
  6. Add soy sauce on top, then season with white pepper, MSG, garlic salt, and sesame oil to taste.
  7. Combine the rice and chicken in one container.
