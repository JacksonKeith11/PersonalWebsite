Board Brawl

A spooky party board game for you and your friends. Roll around Haunted Hollow, buy crowns, set traps and rob each other blind. After every round, everyone plays a minigame for coins. Most crowns wins.

How to play
  • Two to four players, each on their own phone or computer. Bots can fill empty seats.
  • Each round, everyone takes a turn: use one item if you like, then roll a 1–10 die and walk.
  • Blue spaces give 3 coins, red ones take 3. ? spaces do something random, chests give an item, shops sell them, crossed bones start a duel, and portals whisk you across the board.
  • When you pass the crown, buy it for 20 coins. Then it moves somewhere else.
  • Be mean: traps, webs, curses, hexes, pickpockets, zombie hands and pumpkin bombs. A magic mirror bounces the next one back.
  • After every round there's a minigame: free-for-all, 2 v 2, or one against everyone.
  • It helps whoever's behind: the ghost hunts the leader, shops are half price for the back half, and the last three rounds are the Witching Hour.
  • At the end, bonus crowns go to whoever won the most minigames, held the most coins, and took the most from rivals. Most crowns wins; coins break ties.
