Games

Roguelike Tactics Pixel-art strategy

Play in browser

Lead a warrior, a mage and a rogue through three acts of turn-based battles on a pixel-art grid. Collect artifacts that change your skills, and promote your heroes after each boss.

Works with a mouse or a touchscreen. Runs save in your browser between visits.

Frag Arena Multiplayer arena shooter

Play in browser

A fast multiplayer shooter: everyone on the page drops into the same arena. Rockets, rocket jumps, four maps and a few party modes, with bots if no one else is around.

Needs a keyboard and mouse. Best with friends on at the same time.

Board Brawl Party board game for friends

Play in browser

A spooky party board game for two to four friends, each on their own device. Race for crowns, sabotage each other, and play a minigame at the end of every round.

Works on phones and computers. Best with everyone on a call. Bots can fill empty seats.

Saltbound Co-op strategy RPG for 1 to 3 players

Play in browser

A co-op strategy RPG across a sinking archipelago. Pick three heroes, explore a hex map, and fight with dice before the rising sea wins.

Best on a computer, with friends on a call. Make a room and share the code, or play all three heroes yourself.

Song Guesser Name that tune

Play in browser

Name the song from a synthesized clip. It starts with just the drums, and each wrong guess adds another instrument.

Turn your sound on. Everything you hear is synthesized in the browser.
