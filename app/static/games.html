Threefold Path Tactical roguelike

Play in browser

Lead a warrior, a mage and a rogue through three acts of branching battles, strange events and shops. Fights play out on a pixel-art tactics grid with walls, lava and high ground; upgrades rewrite your skills in odd ways, and each act boss lets a hero become something stranger. Built with Phaser, with chiptune music made in code.

Works with a mouse or a touchscreen. Runs save in your browser between visits.
