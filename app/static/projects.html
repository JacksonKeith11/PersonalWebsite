Myopia Prediction Chrome extension for RevolutionEHR

Source on GitHub

A Chrome extension for eye doctors that adds a Myopia Prediction button to the open patient in RevolutionEHR. It reads the patient's age, sex and past eyeglass prescriptions and charts each eye's projected nearsightedness: untreated progression at age- and sex-based rates until it levels off in the late teens, the range expected with treatment, and the patient's own trend from their prescription history.

Everything runs in the browser in plain JavaScript and SVG. Patient data is read from the page, never stored or sent anywhere, and the extension never changes the record. It is being trialled in an optometry office.
