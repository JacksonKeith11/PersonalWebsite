Sketch Relay

A drawing game for three to eight friends. Write something, pass it on, draw what you're handed, guess what the last person drew. At the end, read back how badly it all went.

How to play
  • Three to eight players, each on their own phone or computer.
  • Everyone starts by writing something to draw.
  • Every step, what you made is passed to the next player. Handed words, you draw them. Handed a drawing, you write what you think it is.
  • It carries on until everyone has added a page to every chain.
  • Then each chain is read out from the start, one page at a time.
  • There are no points. Drawing badly is the point.
